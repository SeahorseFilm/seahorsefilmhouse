Tokyo (working title)

Some destinations don’t set you free

StatusIn development
Directed byR P Khemanand
Running timec. 95 min
LanguagesJapanese / English / Hindi

A woman in Tokyo buys a gun on a Tuesday afternoon and hires a private driver for a day’s journey to Nagoya. She does not tell him what is in her bag. She does not tell him whose house they are driving to.

Images shown are concept artwork; the film has not yet been shot.
