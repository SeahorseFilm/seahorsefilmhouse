6 December 2019

Dark Light released in the United States

Dark Light was released in the United States on 6 December 2019, distributed by Vertical Entertainment, according to Box Office Mojo’s listing for the film.

The release followed the film’s world premiere at Arrow Video FrightFest in London that August.

Source: Box Office Mojo
