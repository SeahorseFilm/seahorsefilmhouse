Chasm is coming in Winter 2026

Chasm, the next film from Seahorse Filmhouse, is set for Winter 2026.

The film is an atmospheric portrait of Ila, a woman on the edge of quiet transformation, and a soulful exploration of the space between silence, mystery, and becoming.

More details will be posted on the Chasm page as the release approaches.
