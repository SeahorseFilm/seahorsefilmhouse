Winter Dreams: a Fitzgerald story for today

Seahorse Filmhouse is developing Winter Dreams, a modern reimagining of F. Scott Fitzgerald’s short story.

Set in today’s world, it follows ambition and desire as they blur the line between success and self-loss: a story of chasing dreams, loving illusions, and waking up too late.
