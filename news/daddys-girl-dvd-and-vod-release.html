23 September 2020

Daddy’s Girl arrives on DVD and on demand

Rue Morgue has announced the North American home release of Daddy’s Girl, with the DVD out on 29 September 2020 and video on demand following on 2 October, through Cleopatra Entertainment.

The magazine notes that director Julian Richards won the Best Director prize at the Fantasporto International Film Festival for the film. The cast is led by Jemma Dallender and Costas Mandylor, from a screenplay by Timothy Hill and Sean Hogan.

Source: Rue Morgue, 23 September 2020
